NEXA-TUTOR-001 and NEXA-ORCH-001 remain Baseline Draft. Behavior and Tutor Generation control have headless bindings. Tutor evidence proves only exact control-request acceptance and completion/join of the owned control task, not that `LanguageModelProvider::generate` or provider work stopped. Retrieval has an async cooperative service foundation and its separate headless binding; cancellation proves only service-future termination. Speech, tool/lab, renderer, concrete provider/network cancellation, persistence, clock, observability, interruption, timeout, retry, and recovery integrations remain absent.
