NEXA-TUTOR-001, NEXA-SPCH-001, and NEXA-ORCH-001 remain Baseline Draft. Behavior and Tutor Generation control have headless bindings. Tutor evidence proves only exact control-request acceptance and completion/join of the owned control task, not that `LanguageModelProvider::generate` or provider work stopped. Retrieval has an async cooperative service foundation and its separate headless binding; cancellation proves only service-future termination. Speech has cancellation-control and four-surface composite-coordinator foundations plus the separate ADR-0067 asynchronous input-port foundation; ADR-0064 binds complete coordinator evidence with correlated Behavior evidence, proving only owned control futures/tasks terminalized. Concrete external Speech cancellation, Tool Execution and sandbox implementations, renderer, concrete provider/network cancellation, persistence, clock, observability, interruption, timeout, retry, and recovery integrations remain absent.
